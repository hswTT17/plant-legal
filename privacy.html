2.1 Where these records live

  • On your phone, in a local database. Nowhere else.
  • We operate no server that receives them. There is no account to create and no cloud sync.
  • Predictions are calculated on your device. Cycle length, ovulation estimates and fertile windows never leave the phone.
  • Notifications are generated on your device. No push service is involved, so no reminder content is transmitted.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs.

Because we never receive these records, we cannot read them, cannot restore them, and cannot hand them to anyone — including authorities — no matter who asks.

2.2 These are sensitive records

Menstrual cycles, attempts to conceive, pregnancy, contraception and intimate activity are treated as special category data under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (Article 9), and as sensitive information (민감정보) under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (Article 23). Comparable protections apply in many other jurisdictions.

Our position on these records is the same as for everything else listed above, and it is the reason the app is built the way it is:

  • We do not collect them. They are written to a database on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any processor.
  • We therefore cannot disclose them. Not to advertisers, not to researchers, not to authorities — regardless of who asks or under what authority. We do not hold a copy.
  • Every field is optional. Recording intimate activity, pregnancy, or anything else is entirely your choice, and choosing not to does not limit the rest of the app.
  • You are asked before any of it is stored. The app requests your explicit consent to process health data during setup, separately from accepting the terms of service. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your data in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Because these records never leave your device, the safeguards that matter most are the ones on the device itself. See section 10.

5. Backups are deliberately turned off

Android normally copies app data to your Google account automatically. We have disabled this for PlanT (allowBackup="false") so that your health records are not uploaded to Google's servers without you asking.

What this means for you: if you change phones, uninstall the app, or clear its data, your records are gone. There is no copy anywhere else for us to restore. Use Settings → My data → Export before you switch devices.

7. Retention

Health records are kept on your device until you delete them. We hold none of them, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Subscription records held by our processor are retained for as long as needed to service your subscription and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

8. International transfers

RevenueCat and Google process data on servers that may be located outside your country, including the United States. This applies only to the subscription information described in section 3. Health records are not transferred anywhere because they are not transmitted at all.

9. Children

PlanT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app stores health records only on the device, we have no means of identifying the age of any user. If you are a minor, please use the app with the involvement of a parent or guardian where that is appropriate for you.

10. Security

Your records are protected by your device's own security — screen lock, disk encryption, and Android's application sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading PlanT's database. Keeping your device locked and updated is the most effective protection. Network traffic for subscription management is encrypted in transit.

11. This is not medical advice

PlanT provides estimates based on the dates you enter. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and must not be used as a method of contraception. Predictions can be wrong, particularly if your cycles are irregular. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
